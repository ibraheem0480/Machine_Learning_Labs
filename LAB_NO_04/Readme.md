Lab 04 (Parts 1 and 2) from the University of Engineering and Technology Peshawar (Nowshera Campus) for the Machine Learning Lab course, focusing on feature extraction and advanced correlation analytics for the American Electric Power (AEP) hourly energy consumption dataset. The workflow systematically expands the dataset by extracting core time features, including day of the year, hour, month, day of the week, weekends, and dummy-encoded seasonal variables representing winter, spring, summer, and fall to capture cyclical consumption behaviors (Part 1). It then subjects these engineered characteristics to a multi-method statistical audit, utilizing Pearson, Kendall, and Spearman rank correlation coefficients to evaluate linear, non-linear, and monotonic relationships between time, seasonal shifts, and power grid load variations (Part 2).
